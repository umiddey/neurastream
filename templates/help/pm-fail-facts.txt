pm-fail-facts: every fact about a Playwright run's failures in ONE call.

Replaces hand-grepping logs/artifacts turn by turn (owner 2026-10-11: deterministic work goes to scripts).
For each failed test in <worktree>/test-results/e2e-junit.xml: title, file:line, error head,
artifact dir, error-context.md head, failed-requests.log, console errors; then backend log errors.
Usage: pm-fail-facts <worktree> [--backend-log PATH] [--lines N]
