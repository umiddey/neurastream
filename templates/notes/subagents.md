# Subagents

`## Allowed` is machine-read. A spawn name is allowed only when it appears as a backticked slug on its own bullet. Unlisted pins stay installed but cannot be spawned.

- `glm`
- `deepseek`
- `composer`
- `hiko`
- `muse`
- `luna`
- `sol`
- `grok`
- `gemini`
- `local-qwen`
- `haiku`
