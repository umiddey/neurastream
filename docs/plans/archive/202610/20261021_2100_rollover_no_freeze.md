## Background
- Why it freezes: `main-rollover` (index.ts:435) calls `mainStartText`, which runs six children with `spawnSync` (main-start.ts:19-55). omp's TUI and the command share one JS thread, so nothing repaints and no key is handled until all six finish (~90 s live). `agent-ledger` runs twice (`--hours`, `--cap`) and each reads all 12 GB of sessions (P1).
- Why the notice matters: the owner's typed message leaves the editor when it becomes `/main-rollover <msg>`; without a notice it looks lost.
- The rollover state rules (rollover.ts:31-40) and the input rewrite (index.ts:406-414) are correct and stay as they are.
